## Findings

### A1 · Final admission needs one commit boundary

**High · Amend before accepting the versioned admission contract.** The evaluator can verify a current result and stop revision, and the decision owner can commit a reservation after either has changed. Both can satisfy their own reading of the current checklist. The attempt store's current decision validator verifies the epoch and lock holder. An off-site actor can update the stop independently and an upstream deadline can pass independently. The current re-read of the production head cannot substitute for a specified stop-revision and freshness guard.

Define the authoritative start as the signed conditional admission transition, including the reservation under staging policy v2. Require the transition to consume the exact final result and applicable stop revision while valid at that boundary. A newer stop before this boundary refuses the attempt. A changed input or reached currency deadline before this boundary requires re-evaluation. A store retry must repeat the checks or refuse. Keep later-stop behavior and the no-reservation pre-admission refusal disposition. Exercise both orderings of a concurrent stop and the deadline-before-commit case. ### A2 · The active and candidate resolved-profile subjects can differ in one environment

**High · Bind each reader to its exact subject.** The owner resolved digest includes release artifacts, the environment, catalog and live binding sections. Different baseline and candidate releases in the same environment can share an invariant infrastructure digest and have different resolved digests. The currency reader could bind the running baseline, while the promotion writer could verify the candidate's planned render. Both can interpret the generic production profile as their valid subject. A first installation has live providers but no deployed baseline application.

Define the observed installation and ratified comparison identity used by the currency check. Bind the candidate's own canonical resolved profile to its exact intended render and verify its established environment inputs. Preserve the existing baseline record and its own exact resolved identity. At bootstrap, exercise a ratified profile and live providers with an otherwise eligible first candidate, without requiring previously deployed application workloads or candidate production records. Demonstrate equal infrastructure digests and distinct valid resolved digests for baseline and candidate. ### A3 · The recovery-history verifier needs the exact foundation root

**Medium · Specify the producer and trust chain.** The spine correctly refuses an unknown history and permits a verified first empty environment. A bootstrap builder can satisfy Story 6.7 with signed attempt effects without issuing or identifying the target-lineage root a Story 6.10 or 6.18 verifier expects. Use the successful signed Story 6.7 foundation attempt as the lineage root. Bind the target and initial state to it and require the AR-44 reviewers to bind the verified lineage history and report dispositions in their signed actions. ### A4 · Isolated instances need a drill-scoped comparison source

**Medium · Specify how derivative targets get their comparison inventory.** The isolated control job and policy's fail-closed behavior are clear. The job may still use production instance IDs and pairing from the ratified profile, while the drill preparer gives substitute instances different scoped identities. Owner v2 requires exact IDs and separate independently enumerated coverage. This can make a healthy and fully observed drill fail by construction or invite an unauthorized alternative production inventory.

Require Story 8.17 to prepare a drill-only binding from the ratified pins, an independently enumerated scoped inventory and the actual drill pairing. Keep both target identity and any substitutions explicit. Missing applicable coverage or pairing produces a retained failure. The production inventory and authorization flow remain authoritative only for production.
