# Cross-team architecture review

**Changes required. Five high findings and one medium finding.** The November 27 plan breaks the evaluator and drill sequencing cycle. The producer and consumer contracts still permit opposite decisions about the first release, profile qualification, and recovery evidence.

Reviewed the November 27 working-tree spine over `f87f1593ca07ce617df50afb745a1995fde4b1e0` against the adopted policies, Story 4.9, Story 9.2, and the epic acceptance criteria. The scenarios below are design counterexamples. [Reviewed spine](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md)

## High findings

### ADV-1 · First production admission can reject the approved empty or degraded path

**High · Autofix.** The spine explicitly permits a signed one-attempt approval of the observed production stop and makes eligible empty or degraded production the exception to the working-baseline health check. Story 6.14 tells its implementer to apply a clear-stop condition and require a ready working baseline with passing smokes for each production release. Story 6.23 expects that same admission workflow to accept the first approved installation. The first-release workflow can correctly supply approved empty-state and currency evidence while the admission workflow refuses because there is no working baseline to check. A subsequent eligible degraded repair can also fail on the existing stop. [Spine, Release modes and empty or degraded production, lines 342–343](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#release-and-recovery-acceptance) · [Spine, admission preconditions 1 and 8, lines 370 and 377](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#production-preconditions) · [Story 6.14 acceptance, lines 3614–3625](../../../../epics.md#story-614-refuse-production-attempts-that-fail-a-precondition) · [Story 6.23 first approved release, lines 3918–3920](../../../../epics.md#story-623-deploy-the-reference-release-to-production-with-ingress-closed-and-reach-g1)

Make Story 6.14 state both exact exceptions. Exercise the first approved release with no baseline and an approved repair with an observed stop revision. Exercise refusal when the exception is inapplicable or the stop advances before start.

### ADV-2 · Profile consumers can use incompatible digests

**High · Autofix.** The spine names a singular profile digest in its conventions, precondition 6, and rollout record. Story 4.9 binds the separately adopted v2 infrastructure and resolved digest domains. The staging admission team can compare the structural infrastructure identity while the production rollout team uses the complete resolved identity for the same `profile digest` field. Otherwise equivalent staging and production have different resolved digests when their current bindings differ. Conversely a writer could supply the reusable infrastructure digest where EventStore expects exact resolved bytes. EventStore's promotion authority checks both digest domains. The currency policy adds an `active profile digest` field that now needs the same explicit interpretation. [Spine, production profile, line 254](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#consistency-conventions) · [Spine, rollout, line 353 and precondition 6, line 375](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#release-and-recovery-acceptance) · [Hosted profile v2, lines 75–94](../../../../../../docs/contracts/hosted-profile-binding-v2.md#exact-encoding-and-distinct-identities) · [Story 4.9, lines 26–30](../../../../../implementation-artifacts/spec-4-9-ratify-the-hosted-runtime-and-identity-event-contracts.md#boundaries--constraints) · [EventStore publication authority, lines 185–196](../../../../../../references/Hexalith.EventStore/docs/architecture/publication-authority-v1.md) · [Currency result inventory, line 247](../../../../../../docs/contracts/shared-infrastructure-currency-policy-v1.md#required-result-record)

Link the adopted v2 encoding and name both field identities at the producer/consumer seam. Use infrastructure identity for environment comparability and apply EventStore's exact promotion identity requirements where a record grants authority. State which identities currency evidence, qualification and recovery verify. Exercise equal infrastructure with differing current bindings and refusal of exchanged digest domains.

### ADV-3 · Baseline qualification renewal is absent from the executable admission checklist

**High · Autofix.** The profile convention requires both renewed production baseline records after an environment-layer or shared-infrastructure change. Numbered precondition 6 and Story 6.14 check the candidate's qualified sets or staging qualification against current versions, without requiring the two production baseline records. Assume the candidate has fresh staging qualification at the new digest, but the baseline's production qualification or promoted record is missing. The infra workflow views the baseline as unqualified and the admission workflow can still accept the candidate, even though the compatibility and rollback branch relies on that baseline. A mode-independent currency pass confirms versions and pairing and leaves this separate authority gap open. [Spine, production profile, line 254](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#consistency-conventions) · [Spine, preconditions 6–9 and currency, lines 375–380](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#production-preconditions) · [Story 6.14 precondition 6, line 3622](../../../../epics.md#story-614-refuse-production-attempts-that-fail-a-precondition)

Put the existing baseline-renewal check into precondition 6 and Story 6.14. Bind both to the exact affected working-baseline release and active profile, with applicability explicit. Keep candidate authority issuance at rollout. Qualify refusal for either missing renewed baseline record after a profile change while the candidate and its staging evidence are current.

### ADV-4 · Currency result retention has no shared authority contract

**High · Discuss before 6.14 implementation.** Policy v1 specifies which identities and observations to retain but leaves the result encoding and evaluator to Story 9.2. The spine defines trusted writers for release, attempt, qualification and Administrator records and requires signed records from those writers. It adds a retained currency result as an authority-bearing admission prerequisite without specifying its authorized writer or how admission proves it retained the exact result. Story 9.2 also expressly refuses bare files or digests as authentication. A checker team can emit complete JSON to workflow artifacts and an executor team can require an authenticated attempt record from the off-cluster store. Both can implement the named result fields while disagreeing on admissibility and records available to the G3 evidence consumer. [Spine, record writers, line 249](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#consistency-conventions) · [Spine, currency admission, line 380](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#production-preconditions) · [Spine, shared record encodings, lines 530–531](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#first-shared-versions) · [Policy v1 result contract, lines 234–257](../../../../../../docs/contracts/shared-infrastructure-currency-policy-v1.md#required-result-record) · [Story 9.2 constraints, lines 29–33](../../../../../implementation-artifacts/spec-9-2-run-the-shared-infrastructure-currency-check.md#boundaries--constraints)

Agree the writer, verifying principal, durable retention boundary and successful-retention proof for each trigger. At admission, require a trusted executor-bound record or another explicit authenticated handoff that binds the result to the current attempt and rejects missing or unverifiable evidence. A summary or artifact digest without its trusted producer is insufficient. Preserve the rule that 9.2 evaluates currency and 6.14 owns the admission decision.

### ADV-5 · AR-44 lacks a durable test for when a missing lost-window report is permissible

**High · Discuss before 6.10/6.18 implementation.** Recovery records the lost window at verification and reports it on reopening. Clearance and approval must distinguish a recovery capable of producing a lost window whose record is missing from no such recovery. A recovery writer can persist an entry, restore to the earlier cut, and be interrupted before recording a report. A clearance builder can interpret `has occurred` as a completed recovery and mark the review inapplicable. Another builder can interpret it as the recorded recovery entry and refuse until the report is reconciled. Once an operator makes production working again, the same persisted history can support opposite verdicts. The architecture also needs a common way to associate applicable recoveries and reports with the observed production stop revision. [Spine, promotion stop and lost-window handling, lines 356 and 363](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#release-and-recovery-acceptance) · [Spine, recovery steps 1 and 4–7, lines 386–392](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#recovery-sequence) · [AR-44, line 518](../../../../epics.md#additional-requirements) · [Story 6.10, lines 3516–3521](../../../../epics.md#story-610-keep-a-durable-per-cause-promotion-stop)

Have recovery persist a trusted applicability marker before its first potentially lossy action. Link applicable reports and their disposition to the recovery, affected production instance and relevant stop causes. Require a known history for `not applicable` and refuse unknown applicability or missing evidence from a qualifying interrupted recovery. Include baseline re-deploy, first installation, and interrupted data restore in the classification examples.

## Medium finding

### ADV-6 · The drill check does not identify the inspected deployment and observation boundary

**Medium · Discuss before integrating 8.17.** The drill is isolated and its restored workloads are egress-denied. The evaluator needs exact deployed identities and current authoritative upstream observations for each invocation. The drill contract says only `checked deployment and inventory`. A drill implementer can inspect the isolated replacement environment with declared substitutions, while an evaluator owner can attach a live production/shared profile observation to that drill. These are different deployment claims, and a checker installed inside the drill's denied workload network cannot fetch its authoritative inputs. The drill's result can fail closed without compromising live production, yet G3 can count evidence describing the wrong topology. [Spine, disaster recovery evidence, line 361 and drill handoff, line 568](../../ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md#deferred-and-qualification-work) · [Policy v1 cadence, lines 58–85](../../../../../../docs/contracts/shared-infrastructure-currency-policy-v1.md#2-cadence-and-triggers) · [Policy v1 comparisons, lines 189–223](../../../../../../docs/contracts/shared-infrastructure-currency-policy-v1.md#comparison-and-fail-closed-evaluation) · [Story 8.17, substitutions, isolation and currency result, lines 4645–4652 and 4668–4671](../../../../epics.md#story-817-run-the-isolated-dr-drill-and-prove-rpo-and-rto)

Bind the drill check to the precise observed deployment, inventory and component scopes. Specify how substituted and unavailable components appear in that result and locate read-only collection of current upstream facts outside the restored workloads. Exercise the check on a declared substitution and verify the currency outcome has no block or stop side effect.
