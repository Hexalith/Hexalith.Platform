## Earlier binding content: dropped or weakened

- 30-minute backup runs
- 7-day frequent and 30-day daily retention with complete chains
- 1-hour freshness alert
- 10-minute rollout
- 5-minute verification
- 60 continuous seconds unavailable
- smoke failing twice consecutively, 30 s apart
- missing pass at the deadline fails
- a single probe failure or restart does not fail the window
- 10-minute recovery readiness plus the same window
- no cycling to older releases
- first-deployment handling
- 10-minute startup deadline with finite override
- actual startup duration recorded
- RPO ≤ 1 h and RTO ≤ 4 h
- drills before G2, monthly, and after material changes
- positive production and negative staging access checks
