## Deliberately not raised as new architecture findings

- Exact manifest serialization, gateway metadata fields, release-state storage and run-resource token formats are implementation choices under named producers with validation before consumption. The spine already prevents independent consumers from inventing competing authorities.
- Recovery tooling and the synchronous complete tombstone continuity protocol still require implementation and timed qualification. The spine explicitly assigns that work and does not claim it already exists; demanding its implementation in this document would not improve the architecture contract.
- The user-approved Folders operational override remains governing. Its superseded five-minute RPO, 35-day retention and mandatory replicated topology are not reopened as enrollment blockers. Other module safety and qualification rules remain scoped to their owners.
- Production readiness is not inferred from final architecture status, existing pods, recorded versions or this review. The described evidence gates remain outstanding until proved.
