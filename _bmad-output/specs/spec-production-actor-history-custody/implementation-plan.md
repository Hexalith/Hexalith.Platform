# Dedicated story and implementation tasks

## PLAT-ACTOR-HISTORY-1 — Provide production actor-history custody

As the Platform identity and recovery owner, I want an installed purpose-specific custody provider in the existing owning hosts, so that Party attribution survives profile erasure for its approved finite window and cannot survive expiry through backups or restore.

**Owner:** Hexalith.Platform, with EventStore and Parties integration owners and Administrator/deputy operations. **Scope:** CAP-1–CAP-5. **Status:** specified; backend and operator decisions remain open. This symbolic extension story is not assigned a new epic number by this spec.

### Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-1 — Composition:** All seven SDK methods use real configured dependencies in Parties and EventStore. Admission, retained-source reader/admission, persistence protection, replay/snapshots and owner cleanup resolve the correct provider. Missing/wrong policy, key generation, backend or current authority denies relevant readiness and protected use; ordinary authentication/Consumer behavior is preserved.
2. **AC-2 — Separation and policy:** Event/snapshot attribution remains authorized after profile-key destruction only until original effective-at plus 365 fixed days. At exactly expiry release is denied. Rotation, retries, copying and restoration preserve original evidence/deadline and cannot renew expired content.
3. **AC-3 — Durability and destruction:** Admission, inventory, pending work and original final all-copy receipts survive restart/lost acknowledgement. Every source/derived/backup/key copy becomes irrecoverable; foreign units and still-retained generations are preserved. Concurrent cleanup and late-copy races cannot publish an incomplete receipt.
4. **AC-4 — Restore safety:** An actual pre-expiry data/key/lifecycle backup restored in quarantine cannot revive expired evidence. Current surviving authority, old-incarnation fencing and epoch-bound hooks govern recovery. Unknown lineage or absent authority prevents release/reopening.
5. **AC-5 — Evidence:** The installed-target rows in [test-plan.md](test-plan.md) pass with persisted end-state assertions and retained raw receipts. Local or ReadinessOnly runs cannot satisfy production acceptance. F1/F2 remain explicitly required for full Branch B availability.
