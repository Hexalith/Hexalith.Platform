**Revocability (review loop 3).** The two options above can be revoked in different ways:

- A shared group certificate has no individual certificate revocation mechanism. Removing a shared group binding affects every holder and can affect kubeadm itself; CA rotation has wider effects. The approved individually bound certificate instead loses its authority when its own binding is deleted.
- An unowned ClusterRoleBinding for an individually issued, Administrator-approved identity can be revoked by deleting that one binding. This needs no CA rotation and does not affect kubeadm. The certificate stays valid until it expires, so the deletion removes that identity's authority, not the credential itself.

Only the second option gives each identity its own revocation, which the independent grant/revocation lineage of criterion 6 needs. The Administrator selected sealed kubeadm break-glass custody; its broader revocation effects remain separate from per-identity binding revocation.
