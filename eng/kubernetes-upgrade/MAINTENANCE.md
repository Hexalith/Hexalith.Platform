# Proposed node1 maintenance and recovery procedure

This procedure supplements the [Story 4.1 gate](../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/4-1-upgrade-the-cluster-off-kubernetes-1-34-after-verified-backu.md) and [evidence contract](README.md). It is a concrete proposal for review. It has not been executed or approved as the production procedure. The verified node-file and external-etcd restore rehearsals do not establish complete node reconstruction.

The Administrator has acknowledged the Keycloak, OpenBao, Memories, Forgejo and runner outage, is present as incident commander and recovery owner, and approved an immediate-start window ending **2026-10-02 14:00 Europe/Paris (12:00 UTC)**. This conversation is the incident channel. Codex is the delegated operator. These decisions do not open the technical gate or approve previously unlisted workload changes.

## Scope to approve

The candidate is one hop, observed Kubernetes v1.34.9 to the freshly resolved current v1.35 patch. v1.35.9 is the prepared candidate; refresh its support status, binary checksums and image digests at execution. The node uses manually installed binaries. Follow the effective systemd executable paths from the private node inventory rather than introducing apt packages or choosing between the two observed kubelet paths by guesswork. Containerd and external etcd are unchanged during the normal hop; no reboot is proposed.

Fresh read-only controller discovery also found Zot's `registry-distribution/distribution-registry` and `registry-private/private-registry`, `stalwart/stalwart`, `traefik/hexalith-tache-ai-relay`, KubeSphere and infrastructure controllers on the same outage boundary. Approval of this procedure must cover their interruption and restart as well as the five acknowledged services. No KubeSphere removal or upgrade is included. Its documented Kubernetes support ends at 1.34; affirmative compatibility clearance for the selected hop remains a separate prerequisite.

Proposed changes after the signed-open gate are UID-bound replica changes, suspension of the three existing CronJobs, declarative CNPG hibernation, narrowly scoped temporary scheduling tolerations, cordon/drain, the selected Kubernetes binaries/static-pod upgrades and restoration of recorded configuration. Do not modify a live PVC, its binding or reclaim policy. Do not bypass a PodDisruptionBudget, force an eviction or grant a general local-data deletion exception.

If either is missing, stop before any outage or installed-binary change. Permit no new hop after the approved window; a failure invokes incident command, rather than treating the end time as permission to reopen unhealthy services.

## Quiesce to a terminal state

1. The Administrator freezes interactive requests, repository pushes, CI dispatch and direct API operations in the incident channel. Keep intake closed until reconciliation passes. Suspend `openbao/openbao-raft-snapshot`, `hexalith-memories/postgresql-tls-reloader` and `stalwart/stalwart-tls-reloader`, preserving each original value. Require existing Jobs and Forgejo workflows to reach their defined terminal states; queued/running work or an unreadable census closes the transition. Stop `forgejo-runner/Deployment/forgejo-runner` only after running jobs are terminal.
2. For Memories, pause `Deployment/memories-mcp` using the recorded replica count and stop all publishers/callers. Keep `Deployment/memories` running while workflows drain. Apply the checks in the [previously approved backup playbook](../../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/evidence/epic-4/memories-quiescence-resume-playbook.md): two samples at least 60 seconds apart, zero in-flight work, zero stream pending/lag, no unexplained workflow/reminder state and unchanged stream length. That prior approval covers backup operations; this proposal seeks approval for the upgrade transition. Preserve the existing zero replicas of the two access-telemetry Deployments. After terminal drain, stop Memories API pods gracefully.
3. Stop `keycloak/Deployment/keycloak` and `forgejo/Deployment/forgejo` gracefully after their request/job census is terminal. Stop the two registries, Stalwart and the relay after their owners' write/request freeze is recorded. Never terminate an unexplained running transaction or workflow to make the drain pass.
4. While CNPG's operator is still healthy, use declarative hibernation for `keycloak/Cluster/keycloak-postgres`:

   ```bash
   kubectl --context=jpiquot@local -n keycloak annotate clusters.postgresql.cnpg.io keycloak-postgres --overwrite cnpg.io/hibernation=on
   ```

   Require `cnpg.io/hibernation=True`, reason `Hibernated`, no remaining instance pods and unchanged primary/replica PVC identities. This retains all instance PVCs; do not scale `spec.instances` to zero or use imperative hibernation. Use the fully qualified resource name: an unqualified `get cluster keycloak-postgres` failed in this installation. The qualified annotation passed a live server dry-run at 2026-10-01T11:26:50Z; independent before/after reads confirmed the original hibernation annotation, UID and two-instance setting remained unchanged. That check demonstrates admission acceptance only. [CNPG 1.30 procedure](https://cloudnative-pg.io/docs/1.30/declarative_hibernation/).
5. Require bounded Redis/FalkorDB save and persistence checks, then gracefully stop their StatefulSets and telemetry PostgreSQL using their captured replica counts. Require normal termination and retained volumes. Stop OpenBao clients first, then `openbao/StatefulSet/hexalith-keys`; preserve all six data/audit claims and the static-seal/TLS Secrets. No manual seal, new initialization or key rotation is proposed. Record the pre-stop healthy/unsealed raft peer set and commit/applied index without values.
6. After database transitions are complete, stop the remaining non-DaemonSet controllers using the captured exact UID-bound inventory. This includes Dapr placement/scheduler StatefulSets and operator/sentry/injector Deployments, CNPG/Barman, cert-manager, Velero, KubeSphere, Tigera/Calico controllers, CoreDNS, metrics-server and the Local PV provisioner. Preserve all StatefulSet data. Existing zero-replica controllers stay at zero. Any extra live application, operator-driven recreation or unfinished job closes quiescence.
7. Record `maintenance-transition.json` only after every tracked workflow is terminal, the required application pods are absent and every expected pause/hibernate state is observed. A issued scale command alone is insufficient.

## Cordon, drain and hop

1. Cordon `node1` and independently observe `spec.unschedulable=true`. Run bounded drain to terminal exit 0, respecting all budgets. The proposed exceptions are the verified control-plane mirror pods and these exact DaemonSets: `calico-system/calico-node`, `kube-system/kube-proxy`, `kube-system/nodelocaldns`, `traefik/traefik` and `velero/node-agent`. Inventory their actual UIDs at execution. A new pod, unowned pod, local-data warning or eviction refusal stops the transition; do not add `--force`, disable eviction or delete local data to proceed.
2. Only after recorded terminal quiesce, cordon and drain may the operator replace the installed kubeadm with the verified target binary, rerun the exact target plan and apply the selected upstream control-plane upgrade. Record binary/image identities, exits, warnings and actual results. A failed command stops further mutation and retains diagnostics.
3. Upgrade the effective kubelet and kubectl binaries according to the selected Kubernetes instructions, preserving the private binary/recovery inventory. Reload/restart only the required kubelet service. Verify actual API/node/tool versions and skew. Do not start another minor hop.

## Verify while cordoned, then restore service

1. Recover infrastructure controllers in dependency order while applications remain quiesced. For scheduler-managed pods that must run before uncordoning, add only the `node.kubernetes.io/unschedulable` NoSchedule toleration to the previously captured, approved controller templates. Preserve original tolerations, node affinity and security context. Include DNS, CNI controllers, the Local PV provisioner and required admission/operator dependencies. Do not set `nodeName` on the storage probe: it must exercise `WaitForFirstConsumer` scheduling. Do not uncordon to conceal a failed scheduling or storage check.
2. Require API readiness, node Ready, healthy external etcd, CNI/service DNS, restored add-on/operator and admission health, valid skew and cleared target API scans. Run both StorageClass provision/write/independent-read/delete/provider-absence probes again. Any failure stops reopening. Databases/applications intentionally stopped by the recorded transition remain quiesced until this infrastructure gate passes.
3. Uncordon after that gate, independently observe `spec.unschedulable=false`, then restore original infrastructure scheduling configuration and replica counts. Preserve the application intake freeze while restarting dependencies.
4. Rehydrate CNPG by restoring the exact original hibernation annotation; require both instances and database replication health. Restart all three OpenBao voters against their retained data and static seal; require every voter initialized/unsealed and raft quorum. OrderedReady startup must make progress; a stalled first voter invokes the owner instead of deleting data or reinitializing. Start Redis, FalkorDB and telemetry PostgreSQL, verifying persistence/census against the pre-outage evidence, then restart Keycloak and Memories API. Complete login/OIDC, approved OpenBao write/read/delete canary, Memories API/MCP and representative search checks.
5. Restore Forgejo, the additional applications and the runner to their exact captured counts. Require repository/UI health, registry and Stalwart/relay expectations and runner job state to match baseline. Restore the original CronJob suspension values only after their dependencies are healthy. Reconcile every workflow and stream census before reopening callers and Memories MCP. Leave intentionally disabled workloads at zero.
6. Sign `post-upgrade-health.json`, `upgrade-result.json` and the sanitized summary, recording actual versions, every workload outcome and any remaining incident. A failed/unobserved outcome leaves Story 4.1 in progress.

## Failure and recovery decisions

The Administrator remains the accountable recovery owner. Stop further hops, keep intake closed and preserve the failed node/configuration/data and sanitized diagnostics. Attempt diagnosis of the current supported control plane; do not improvise a kubeadm downgrade, replace live PVCs or restore an old etcd snapshot into a running cluster.

The tested file-recovery route independently reads and decrypts the immutable node archive, restores into an isolated target with numeric owner/group/mode/content verification, validates all certificate/key pairs and proves SSH access independently of Kubernetes. The tested etcd route independently reads/decrypts the snapshot and restores a distinct, source-unreachable healthy member with matching source revision/key-count/hash and approved canaries. These are usable diagnostics/recovery inputs, not a tested production OS reconstruction.

If production control-plane/etcd reconstruction is required, preserve diagnostics and invoke a separately approved reconstruction on **identified prepared capacity**. Fence the old API writers/member before starting a restored member. Recover the node configuration/key material with the tested owner/mode/checksum checks. The retained read-only service inventory identifies `etcd.service`, `/usr/local/bin/etcd`, `/etc/etcd.env`, `/var/lib/etcd`, member `node1` and peer `https://192.168.1.30:2380`; client and peer mTLS use `/etc/ssl/etcd/ssl/`. Recheck this inventory and preserve the old data directory and configuration. Use a fresh member/cluster identity and etcdutl 3.6.5 to restore into a distinct recovery directory. The proposed Kubernetes watch-cache policy is a **1,000,000,000 revision bump with `--mark-compacted`**; the recovery owner must confirm it exceeds all observed client revisions before use. Restart Kubernetes controllers only after restored member health/canaries pass and the old member remains fenced. [etcd 3.6 recovery procedure](https://etcd.io/docs/v3.6/op-guide/recovery/).

Prepared production replacement capacity and a full node reconstruction rehearsal are currently unrecorded. This proposal therefore does not claim that complete reconstruction is ready or authorize destructive restoration. If workload data recovery is needed, use the signed Story 4.0 objects and approved isolated restore procedures into distinct quarantine targets, retain every source PVC, and require a separately approved verified cutover before reopening. Failure of the intact-node restart path escalates here; it is not an instruction to perform an untested restore automatically.
