## Supported measured storage geometry

The transition archive accepts at most 48 MiB of serialized original, preserving the existing 32 MiB full-state upstream maximum and same-ERC/no-backup authority. Shared chunks are 16–128 KiB (final chunk may be smaller), with at most 3,073 chunk pointers. Single-chunk archives retain raw bytes to avoid original/chunk identity collision. Reconstruction needs one transition frame, one raw-state frame and independently hashed chunks; transition-frame recursion is refused. No deletion, eviction, TTL or overwrite grant was added.

The component measurement uses 20 admitted/completed cumulative JSON transitions, growing 32 KiB per transition, alternating beginning/middle inserts and changing a prefix by one byte. Archive counters include unique payloads, transition frames, ancestry metadata and failed prewrites. Its supported bound is `2*(initial+19*32KiB) + 20*384KiB`, or 9,371,648 bytes at 128 KiB initial and 19,595,264 bytes at 5 MiB initial. Exact measured rows are retained in the selected full-suite log. The native measurement repeats eight transitions in both geometries and additionally counts native JSON/base64 write envelopes; its bound is `3*(initial+7*32KiB) + 8*768KiB`. These measured bounds establish the tested geometry, not infinite capacity or physical-copy erasure.
